A clearer day. A little less to keep in your head.

DotReminder by DotSuite brings your reminders, calendar and reusable routines together in a clean glass interface.

MAKE EACH REMINDER YOURS
• Choose a date and time, then add a title, notes and a category.
• Repeat daily, weekly or at an interval you choose.
• Personalize sound and vibration, and snooze when you need a little more time.
• Add an optional location for place-based reminders.

SEE WHAT IS AHEAD
• Browse, search and filter your reminders by category or status.
• Plan upcoming days in the calendar.
• Organize categories with colors and icons, and save templates for tasks you repeat.
• Choose a light or dark appearance and sync saved account data after signing in.

AI HELP, WITH YOU IN CONTROL
Describe what you want to remember in your own words. The optional assistant can suggest a reminder draft; review and edit the title, time and details before saving. AI needs an internet connection and can misunderstand a request.

YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR CONTROL
• Sign in with email, Google or Facebook.
• SMS code verification during email-and-phone registration.
• Authenticator-based two-step verification for eligible accounts.
• Biometric app lock for your signed-in session on supported devices.
• Clear links to privacy, support and account/associated-data deletion requests.

Allow notifications and exact alarms when requested so reminders can work as intended. Device power settings, Do Not Disturb and location permissions can affect alerts or sound. Includes ads.
